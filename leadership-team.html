DG

Damian Grzywacz, MHA

in
Founder & CEO

Damian grew up in Rochester, NY, after being born in a Polish neighborhood in Chicago, IL. He attended SUNY-Potsdam and the University of Georgia and went on to Seton Hall University for his Masters of Healthcare Administration. He has been in the Healthcare arena since 2003 and has held leadership positions within Maxim Health, Hospital Corporation of America (HCA) and Universal Health Services (UHS). Damian co-founded DrCruiter, a leader in Permanent Staffing Solutions. He currently sits on the Advisory Board for Healthcare Perspectives, a collaboration of information exchange for providers, healthcare groups and hospital systems. With his passion and enthusiasm for making a difference in Healthcare, he founded Korzen Health in 2019. Damian currently resides in Atlanta, Georgia.

KM

Kent McMackin, PhD

in
Founder & President Physician Leaders for Today

Kent retired from the United States Navy after serving 23 years in Navy Medicine as a hospital administrator. He would go on to serve as a Chief Executive Officer in both for-profit and not-for-profit hospitals as well as in senior executive leadership roles, at the corporate level, for HCA, Acadia Healthcare, and Cogent HMG, at that time the largest for-profit management service organization in the United States. For the last 11 years Kent has served as the Founder/President of Physician Leaders for Today, a company that focuses on developing and advancing physicians as leaders and consultants in the healthcare industry. He did his undergraduate work in Healthcare Services at Southern Illinois University; graduate work in Human Resource Management Development at Chapman University and post graduate work in Organizational Psychology at Walden University.

Ed Speedling, MHA

in
CEO Advisor - ES Healthcare Consulting

Ed is a seasoned healthcare leader with a passion for transformative change in the mental health arena.
